---
title: Scratchpad
description: Keep plain-text notes for a Saved Connection or for the whole app.
---

Scratchpad stores user-authored plain text locally. Each Saved Connection has one note, and one global note is shared across all Saved Connections and Workspaces.

Notes are size-bounded and autosave after a short editing delay. Closing a Workspace does not delete its connection note or the global note. You can edit, undo, redo, and delete a note from the Scratchpad view.

Control Room never captures terminal output, logs, passwords, or commands into Scratchpad automatically. Notes are ordinary local data, not encrypted secret storage.
