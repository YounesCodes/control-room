**Logs** reads recent lines from a service or container, and can keep following new ones.

The **Warnings** and **Errors** filters find lines by words such as `warning`, `error`, or `failed`, or by a level written in the line, such as `level=error`.

The view keeps the newest 10,000 lines. Logs are never saved. Closing the view stops the stream.

## Where logs come from

- **Linux.** Services come from the systemd journal (`journalctl`). Containers come from `docker logs`.
- **Windows.** Container logs only. Windows event logs are not shown.
- **macOS.** Choose **Unified log** for the system log, or pick a launchd service.

If the account can't read the journal, use **Retry with sudo**, or add the account to the `systemd-journal` group on the host.
