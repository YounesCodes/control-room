In **Logs**, choose a service or container, choose how many recent lines to load, then select **Start**. Turn on **Receive new lines** to keep reading as new lines arrive.

- **Warnings** and **Errors** show only those lines. Control Room spots them by words such as `warning`, `error`, or `failed`, or by a level written in the line, such as `level=error`.
- Press `Ctrl+F` to search the loaded lines.
- **Pause** holds new lines while you read. **Resume** shows them.
- Scroll up to read older lines. **Jump to latest** takes you back to the newest.
- **Stop** ends the stream.

The view keeps the newest 10,000 lines. Logs are never saved. Closing the view stops the stream.

## Where logs come from

- **Linux.** Services come from the systemd journal (`journalctl`). Containers come from `docker logs`.
- **Windows.** Container logs only. Windows event logs are not shown.
- **macOS.** Choose **Unified log** for the system log, or pick a launchd service.

If the account can't read the journal, Logs says so. Select **Retry with sudo**, or add the account to the `systemd-journal` group on the host.
